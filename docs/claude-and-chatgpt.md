# Claude and ChatGPT

Use Vendi inside Claude or ChatGPT, or answer with your ChatGPT plan.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/claude-and-chatgpt

## Use Vendi in Claude or ChatGPT (Pro)

Ask about your meetings, CRM and other apps from inside Claude or ChatGPT. Open **Integrations › Use Vendi in Claude or ChatGPT** and press **Set up Claude** or **Set up ChatGPT**. The guide ticks off each step as you go.

- **Claude.** In Claude, open **Customize › Connectors › Add custom connector**. Name it Vendi, paste the address from the guide, press **Connect**, then **Allow**. On Team and Enterprise plans an owner adds it once for everyone.
- **ChatGPT.** Needs a paid ChatGPT plan with developer mode on. Add a custom server named Vendi, keep the default sign-in and press **Allow**. Then type `@Vendi` in a chat.

Changes asked from Claude or ChatGPT wait for you in [CRM Updates](approvals.md), like any other change, unless Autopilot is on. Deletes, emails and invites always wait. Tools you add through Executor follow Executor's own approvals.

## Answer with your ChatGPT plan

If you pay for ChatGPT, Vendi can use it for Smart answers. Press **Connect ChatGPT**, approve the short code at OpenAI and keep the page open until it connects. When ChatGPT is busy, Vendi switches back to its own model.
