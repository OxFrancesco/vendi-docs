# Integrations

Every app Vendi can read and work in.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/integrations

Open **Integrations** at the bottom of the sidebar, press **Connect** and sign in to the app. Vendi sees only what your account sees there. If an app needs you again, its row says **Sign in again**.

## Scheduling (Pro)

- Google Calendar
- Calendly

With Google Calendar, Vendi works on the calendars you own and finds times that work for everyone, in their own time zone. It books, moves or cancels meetings with a Google Meet link, and each change waits for your approval. For calendars others shared with you, it only sees when they're busy. Check your own time zone with `/timezone`.

With Calendly, Vendi books people into your open times. On Calendly's free plan it makes a one-time booking link instead.

## Any other app (Pro)

Not on the list? There are three ways in, easiest first:

1. **Ask Vendi**, like "connect Attio". It knows many more apps, like Gmail, Drive, Sheets, Outlook, Close, monday.com, ClickUp, Stripe and Zapier, and gives you a sign-in button. Some sign you in to Executor first, then the app, in the same tab. Tools you add through Executor follow Executor's own approvals.
2. **Add an MCP server**, if the app offers one. Its help pages will say so.
3. **Add a website**, for anything else. You log in once in Vendi's browser. Vendi never sees your password. Anything that would send or change something waits for your approval.

Tip: Team admins can limit each person to read-only or no access, per app. See [Team and access](team.md).
