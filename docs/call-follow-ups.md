# Never miss a follow-up

Every call scored and the follow-up drafted, by itself.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/call-follow-ups

About 10 minutes to set up.

## What you need

A notetaker connected, like Granola. See [Integrations](integrations.md#meetings).

## Steps

1. **Score your last call.** On [Today](today.md), press **Review my last call**. Vendi scores it from 0 to 100 and says what the next step should be.
2. **Draft the follow-up.** On the scorecard, press **Draft follow-up email**. Vendi writes it from what was said on the call. Edit what you like, then send it.
3. **Make the next step a task.** Press **Make it a task** so it has an owner and a due date in the [CRM](ttcrm.md).
4. **Do it for every call.** Ask Vendi: "After each meeting, score the call and draft the follow-up." It becomes an [automation](automations.md) that runs as soon as your notetaker's notes arrive.
5. **Check Today each morning.** Calls that ended with a vague or no next step show on Today with **Draft follow-up**. Clear that list and nothing slips.

## Get better at calls

Open **CRM › Calls** and look at **How the selling went** on a few scorecards. Weak spots link to the matching play in the [Sales Method](playbook.md#sales-method). Managers can open **Whole team** to compare sellers.

Tip: Calls land on the right company by themselves, from the people invited or the meeting title. If one doesn't, press **Link a company**.
