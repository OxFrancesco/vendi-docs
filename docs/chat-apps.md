# Chat apps

Message Vendi from WhatsApp, Telegram, iMessage, Slack or Discord.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/chat-apps

Link one in **Integrations › Messaging**.

## Link an app

- **WhatsApp and iMessage.** Enter your phone number, then send the message Vendi prepares from that phone.
- **Telegram.** Press **Connect**, then **Start** in the bot.
- **Slack.** Press **Connect** and approve the install. Commands start with `/vendi`, as in `/vendi help`.
- **Discord.** Press **Connect** and approve. Vendi only reads your direct messages.

## How it works

- Vendi works with all your apps here, like on the web.
- Approve changes with the buttons on each card, or a poll on WhatsApp. With one change waiting, "yes" or "no" works too.
- Lead lists and automation results arrive here when they're ready.
- After a few quiet hours, the next message starts a new conversation. `/new` starts one now.
- Send `/help` for every [command](shortcuts.md#commands). Voice notes work too, see [Voice](voice.md).

## In a group

Add Vendi to a Telegram or WhatsApp group with `/groups`, then mention it or reply to one of its messages. Everyone asks with their own Vendi.

Your calendar, meetings, email and memory stay out of the group; anything private comes to you in a direct message. A change you ask for, like a new Linear issue, waits for your approval in a direct message, never in the group, and what Vendi writes about it goes there too. If Vendi can't reach you there, the group gets one line asking you to message it and send `/changes`. Typing "approve" or "ok" in the group decides nothing. In the direct message, use the buttons or reply "approve" or "dismiss": a plain "ok" only decides a change you asked for in that direct message.

On Autopilot, changes that don't always ask for approval run at once, so there's no approval and Vendi's answer in the group can mention them. Files and photos sent before your question come with it.

Ask for something on a schedule and it posts there, like "every Monday, post a summary of our Renewals page here". These automations read the team's notes, the team's CRM and workspace apps and the web. They don't see what's said in the group, never change records, can't run after a meeting, and post nothing when nothing changed. Vendi tells you privately when you set one up, and whoever is in the group reads every post, including anyone who joins later. They're yours, so you pause or delete them on **Automations**, or by asking in the group. On Telegram one pauses itself if you leave the group, and on any app if Vendi can't post there.

Note: To have Vendi follow your customer groups and find what needs doing, see [Group chats](group-chats.md).
