# Phone apps

Coming soon to iPhone and Android.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/phone-apps

The iPhone and Android apps are coming soon. To hear when they're out, open **Settings › Phone Apps** and press **Join the waitlist**.

Until then, TTFunnel works in your phone's browser, and you can message Vendi from [WhatsApp or Telegram](chat-apps.md).

## What they'll do

- New chats start on voice: tap Vendi to talk. The microphone next to the box dictates instead.
- Voice chats keep going when you switch screens or leave the app.
- Every answer has **Read aloud**.
- The Vendi button in the corner starts a chat about the page you're on.
- The Vendi widget on your home screen starts a chat in one tap.
- On Android, you can set Vendi as your **digital assistant** and get a notification when an answer is ready or something needs your approval.
