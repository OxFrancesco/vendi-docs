# Agents

Vendi and the co-workers, how to ask, and what you can attach.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/chat

## Your agents

You talk to five agents. Each keeps one conversation with you that never starts over, like a chat with a colleague.

| Agent | Handles |
| --- | --- |
| Vendi, your Chief of Staff | Anything you ask, and hands jobs to the others |
| Head of Sales | Call prep, follow-ups and deal reviews |
| Head of Lead Gen | New leads, research and contact details |
| CRM Manager | Deal stages, contacts and a clean CRM |
| Head of Automation | Work that runs on a schedule or after meetings |

They're listed in the sidebar under **Agents**. A coral dot means an unread message. A row says **Working**, **Needs your approval** or **Waiting for your answer** while the agent is busy or needs you. To pin an agent to the top, press **⋯** on its row and choose **Pin to the top**.

On a phone, swipe right to open the sidebar, or pull down from the top of a conversation to switch agents.

## Ask

Open an agent and type. Messages from [Today](today.md), and buttons like **Draft follow-up email**, go to Vendi.

While it works, each step shows above the answer, like "Read Tuesday's call with Acme". When it's done they fold into one line; press it to see them again.

Pick **Fast** for everyday questions or **Smart** (Pro) for harder ones, next to the send button.

## Send while it's answering

You don't have to wait. Enter sends your message after the answer ends. Cmd++Enter adds it to the answer now, and the agent changes what it does next. Choose which one Enter does with **Send after** or **Add now** in **Settings › Messages and Tasks**.

## Fix a message

- **Stop** ends an answer. **Resume** sends any messages still waiting.
- Point at a message you sent and press the back arrow to change it and send it again. Changes already made in your apps stay.
- Select part of an answer and press **Quote** to reply to just those words.

## Find something said before

Press the magnifier (Cmd+F) to search the conversation, or the calendar to jump to a date. Cmd+K searches every agent at once. You can also just ask: "what price did we agree with Halden in May?"

## The + button

Attach files, see which apps are connected, add one of your [SOPs](playbook.md#sops), or start an [automation](automations.md).

## Cards

Some answers come with something to press: a change to approve ([Approvals and undo](approvals.md)), a question with answers to pick (or press 1, 2…), a sign-in for an app, an email to check, or a fact worth saving.

## The agent's panel

The panel button at the top right shows the agent's automations and **Instructions** (what it should always keep in mind), the files and notes in this conversation, and **Computer**, which is coming soon.

**Picture**, under **Details**, swaps the parrot for a plain icon or an image you upload. Team admins can set one for the whole team, and anyone can pick their own. The phone apps still show the parrot.

## Task windows

Turn on **Settings › Messages and Tasks › Run tasks in windows**, then press Cmd+⏎ in Vendi's conversation to send a request as a task in its own small window. Vendi works on several at once and tells you which ones need you.

Tip: What you type is saved as a draft for each agent, so switching agents or reloading doesn't lose it.
