# Group chats

Vendi follows your Telegram groups with customers.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/group-chats

Vendi follows the Telegram groups you share with customers and partners, so requests and promises don't get lost.

## Set it up

1. **Connect the groups.** A team admin connects Telegram groups once for the whole team in **Integrations › Group Chats**. Vendi reads messages from then on, not older ones.
2. **Choose who can read each one.** In **Settings › Group Chats**, the admin sets each group's company and who can read it: the whole team or chosen people. Anyone left out gets nothing from that group.
3. **Get a daily brief.** Press **Set up** under Daily brief for a summary every weekday at 8:30 of what's open, checked against your meetings and CRM.

## What Vendi finds

- **Open items**: unanswered requests, promised follow-ups and possible deals. A teammate's reply closes them.
- **CRM drafts**: changes the messages suggest, waiting for your approval.
- **Urgent alerts**: a private Telegram message when something needs you now. Mute a group with its bell.

You can also just ask: "What did Acme ask for in Telegram this week?"
