# Leads

Describe your buyer and get a checked list of people to contact.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/leads

Note: Leads is part of Pro.

Open **CRM › Leads** and type who you're looking for: roles, companies, size, region and why they might buy now. A search takes one to five minutes, and Vendi tells you in your [chat app](chat-apps.md) when it's ready.

You can ask for leads in a chat too. Vendi shows you who it would look for first, and spends nothing until you say yes. Each search gets its own yes.

Tip: Step by step: [Fill your pipeline every week](weekly-pipeline.md).

## What each lead has

- A fit score from one to five
- **Why now**: dated reasons to reach out, like a new role or a hiring push
- Their current role checked on LinkedIn, and recent posts
- The sources behind every claim

**All leads** shows every lead from every list. Filter by **No deal yet** to see who's new. Use J and K to move between leads.

## What you can do with a lead

- **Start a deal**: adds their company and the deal to the [CRM](ttcrm.md). Select several leads to start one with each.
- **Find emails**: looks up work emails with Apollo or Clay, using your credits there.
- **Remove**: takes someone you're not interested in out of every list, and later searches leave them out as long as that list exists. The trash button on a lead does it, or the menu when you've selected several. **Undo** stays on screen for ten seconds.
- **Find more like these**, **Download CSV** or **Delete list**, from the list menu.

## Weekly lists

Turn on **Every Monday** when you start a list. Each week brings people you haven't seen yet. Open a weekly list on the Leads page for **Run now**, **Change it** and a switch to stop it. On a phone, the list button above the page opens your lists.
