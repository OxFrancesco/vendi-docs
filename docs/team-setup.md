# Set up your team

Invite people, share what Vendi knows and choose who can do what.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/team-setup

Bring your team in so everyone's Vendi knows the same facts and follows the same rules. About 20 minutes, done by an admin.

## Steps

1. **Invite people.** Open **Settings › Members** and invite each person by email. Make anyone who should manage the team an admin.
2. **Each person connects their own apps.** Everyone signs in to their own notetaker and apps. Meetings stay private to each person.
3. **Add team facts.** In [Company Info](playbook.md#company-info), add facts and files under **Team**, like pricing, the product one-pager and who you sell to. Every teammate's Vendi uses them.
4. **Write the team's SOPs.** Add your shared procedures as [SOPs](playbook.md#sops) for **Everyone in this team**, like how you qualify a lead or hand a deal to onboarding.
5. **Set app access.** In [Team and access](team.md#app-access), choose who can change each app, who can only read, and who has no access.
6. **Set CRM permissions.** In the [CRM](ttcrm.md#admin-settings)'s **Settings**, choose whether members see every record or only their own.
7. **Share calls if you want to.** Calls are private by default. Ask each seller to turn on **Share my calls with the team** in Settings › Account, so teammates see them on their companies.

## Optional

- Connect your customer [group chats](group-chats.md) and choose who reads each one.
