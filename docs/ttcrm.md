# CRM

Companies, leads, deals, calls and tasks in one place.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/ttcrm

A CRM built into TTFunnel, for teams that don't have one yet. Its tabs follow a sale: **Companies**, **Leads**, **Deals**, **Calls** and **Tasks**.

## The words

| Word | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Lead | A person you haven't talked to yet, from your lead lists |
| Company | Where a deal happens. **Start a deal** on a lead adds one |
| Contact | A person at a company |
| Deal | One thing you sell to a company, with an amount and a stage |
| Call | A meeting your notetaker recorded, with a score from 0 to 100 |
| Task | Something to do, with an owner and a due date |

## Companies

The list shows each company's deal stage, last call, last group message and last follow-up, with the face of whoever did it. The last call and follow-up show a short summary, with more on hover or tap. Click a stage to move the deal.

Open a company to see everything on one page: its deals, what's left to do, then what happened, newest first. Details and contacts are on the right; click one to change it. **Add** makes a task, note, deal or contact, or links a call.

Note: Your calls are only yours until you turn on **Share my calls with the team** in Settings › Account. Admins always see every call.

## Deals board

The deals list groups deals by stage. Switch to the board for one column per stage, with totals; closed stages fold away. Drag a card to move it. If a stage needs fields that are empty, you fill them first.

## Tasks

Tasks are grouped by due date.

## Views

Search, filter, pick columns and save the result as a view for the team. Views show as tabs above the list.

## Working in a table

Click a value to change it. Select several records to act on them together. Open a record and move to the next or previous one with the arrow keys or J and K. **Ask Vendi** works from a record, and records Vendi mentions in an answer show as live chips.

## Bring your records in

**Import** brings companies, contacts, deals and notes from HubSpot. **Demo data** fills the CRM with made-up records to try it.

## Admin settings

Team admins open **Settings** to add fields, set which fields each stage needs, and choose what members can see and change: everything, only their own records, or nothing.

## History

Every change is kept in the record's history and can be undone. When Vendi changes the CRM from a chat, it asks first, like any other app. Many records for one request come on [one card](approvals.md), and one undo takes them all back. Admins can download a full copy from **Repository**.
