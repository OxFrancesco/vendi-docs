# Onboarding guide

Ten minutes to your first answer, then one thing a day for your first week.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/quickstart

Skip anything you don't need.

## Today: ten minutes

1. **Sign up.** At [ttfunnel.ai](https://ttfunnel.ai), with your name and email. TTFunnel makes a team for you.
2. **Send one message about you.** Who you are, your company and your tools. For example: "Hi, I'm Ana, head of sales at Acme (acme.com). We use Granola, HubSpot and Notion."
3. **Connect your tools.** Vendi answers with a button for each tool you named. Each one opens that app's own sign-in. Start with your notetaker: it's where Vendi learns what happened in your calls.
4. **Press Try it.** Setup ends with a list of what Vendi does. Press **Try it** on any row and Vendi starts it in the same chat.

Tip: **Get started** at the bottom of the sidebar keeps this list for you and ticks each item off as you use it.
