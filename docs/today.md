# Today

Everything that needs you, in one list.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/today

Today is the first page you see. Ask Vendi from the box at the top, or press a suggestion like **Prep my next meeting** or **Review my last call**.

## Needs you

One list of everything waiting on you:

- New leads from your weekly lists
- CRM changes Vendi wants to make
- Updates from your [group chats](group-chats.md)
- This week's calls that ended without a clear next step, with **Draft follow-up**

Filter by **Leads** or **Knowledge**. An empty list means nothing needs you.
