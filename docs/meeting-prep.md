# Prep every meeting

A short brief before each meeting, every morning.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/meeting-prep

Who's coming, what was said last time, and what you promised. About 5 minutes to set up.

## What you need

- Your notetaker connected, for past calls.
- Google Calendar connected (Pro), so Vendi knows what's coming. See [Integrations](integrations.md#scheduling).

## Steps

1. **Try it once.** On [Today](today.md), press **Prep my next meeting**, or ask "Prep me for my 3pm with Acme."
2. **Teach it what matters.** Add your pricing and product facts to [Company Info](playbook.md#company-info), and write how you run a first call as an [SOP](playbook.md#sops). Briefs get sharper with both.
3. **Get a brief every morning.** Ask Vendi: "Every weekday at 8:30, prep me for today's meetings." Link [WhatsApp or Telegram](chat-apps.md) and the brief lands there too.
4. **Ask on the way in.** Send a voice note in [WhatsApp or Telegram](chat-apps.md): "Anything I should know before I walk in?"

## After the meeting

Vendi can book the next one. Ask "Find 30 minutes with Anna next week" and it suggests times that work in both time zones. The invite goes out when you approve it.
