# Playbook

What Vendi knows about your company, you and how you sell.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/playbook

Four tabs. The more Vendi knows, the better its answers.

## Company Info

Facts and files Vendi uses in every answer, like your pricing sheet or a product one-pager. **Add a fact** or **Upload** a file. **Team** facts reach every teammate and only admins edit them; **Only you** facts stay yours. Say "remember this" in a chat to save one.

## Memory

Notes Vendi keeps about you, your people and your deals as you chat. It refreshes them every few hours, or press **Update from chats**. Open a note to edit it; **History** shows what changed.

Deleting a chat also forgets what Memory learned from it. A fact whose only source is that chat goes, and a fact with other sources stays without it. A fact with no source can't be traced to a chat, so remove it here if you want it gone.

## SOPs

Your procedures in plain words, like how you qualify a lead. Give each a name and say when to use it, and Vendi follows it when it fits. Admins can share one with the whole team. You can also ask Vendi to write one for you.

## Sales Method

A built-in guide to running calls, finding the pain, handling objections and keeping deals moving. [Calls and coaching](follow-ups.md) links here when a call shows a weak spot.
