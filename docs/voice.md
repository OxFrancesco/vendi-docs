# Voice

Talk to Vendi instead of typing.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/voice

## Voice chat (Pro)

Press **Talk to Vendi** next to any message box. Vendi listens, answers out loud and shows a short summary on screen. Changes it suggests show in the summary, ready to approve.

The call keeps going while you move around the app, and in Chrome it follows you to other tabs. Say goodbye or press **End the voice chat** to hang up.

## Dictate

The microphone in the message box turns speech into text you can edit before sending.

## Voice notes

Send Vendi a voice note in WhatsApp or Telegram instead of typing. With (Pro), each answer also ends with a short voice note; send `/voice off` to stop them.
