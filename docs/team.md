# Team and access

Members, shared chats and who can use which app.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/team

Everyone in a team shares the plan. Each person connects their own apps, and chats stay private unless someone shares one.

Tip: Step by step: [Set up your team](team-setup.md).

## Members

**Settings › Members** shows who's in your team. Admins invite people by email, make someone an admin, and remove people. Anyone can leave from the menu next to their own name.

When someone leaves or is removed, they lose access at once: open pages close, Claude and ChatGPT lose access, and chat apps are disconnected. Chats they shared are no longer shared. Within a day, their Vendi on that team is deleted for good: their chats, files, memory, automations, change history, backups and app sign-ins. What they added to the team's CRM and playbook stays with the team.

If you invite them back within that day, they get their Vendi back and set up Claude, ChatGPT and chat apps again. After that, they start fresh.

The team's name at the top switches teams, makes a **New team**, and for admins **Rename team**. **Shared chats** lists chats teammates shared with you.

## Deleting your account

**Delete account** in **Profile › Account** deletes your chats, files, memory, automations, change history, backups and app sign-ins for good. You type your email to confirm, and it can't be undone. What you added to your team's CRM and playbook stays with the team. If you're the only person on a team, the team goes too. If you're its only admin and others remain, make someone else an admin first. Sign-ins you gave other companies aren't cancelled on their side, so remove TTFunnel from their app settings if you want that too.

## App access

Admins choose how much each person can do in each CRM and workspace app:

| Level | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Read and change | The default. Changes still need approval. Tools you add through Executor follow Executor's own approvals. |
| Read only | Vendi can look but never change. |
| No access | Vendi doesn't use the app for that person. |

Notetakers aren't affected: each person's meetings are their own.
