# Introduction

An assistant for sales teams that knows your meetings and your CRM.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs

Vendi is the assistant inside TTFunnel. It reads your meeting notes, your CRM and the other apps your team uses, and answers with sources. When something should change, like a CRM field or a follow-up email, Vendi drafts it and waits for your approval.

- **Onboarding guide.** Set up in ten minutes, then a short list for your first week. (https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/quickstart)
- **Tutorials.** Step by step for the jobs that take more than one page. (https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/weekly-pipeline)

## What you can ask

- "What did I promise Anna last week?"
- "Prep me for my 3pm with Acme."
- "Add today's call to the Acme deal."
- "Find 10 heads of sales at Series A fintechs in Italy."
- "Every Monday at 9, recap last week's meetings."

## Three things to know

- **Your own Vendi.** Everyone on a team has one. You connect your apps with your own sign-in, and your chats stay private unless you share one.
- **You approve changes.** Vendi reads freely and doesn't change anything by itself unless you turn on Autopilot, which is off by default. Every CRM edit, email or new record shows up as a card you approve first. Tools you add through Executor follow Executor's own approvals. See [Approvals and undo](approvals.md).
- **It works where you are.** On the web, in WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack and more, and inside Claude or ChatGPT.
