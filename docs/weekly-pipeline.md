# Fill your pipeline every week

A weekly lead list, the best leads turned into deals, and the first emails.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/weekly-pipeline

About 15 minutes to set up. (Pro)

## What you need

- A lead source connected, like Apollo or Clay, if you want work emails. See [Integrations](integrations.md#leads).

## Steps

1. **Describe your buyer.** Open **CRM › Leads** and write who you want, like a colleague would: "Heads of sales at fintechs in Italy, 20 to 200 people, hiring sellers now." Role, company type, size, region and a reason to buy now give the best list. Vendi asks if something important is missing.
2. **Turn on Every Monday.** Before you start the search, turn on **Every Monday**. From then on, each week brings people you haven't seen yet.
3. **Read the list.** The first list is ready in one to five minutes; Vendi tells you in your chat app. Start with fit score 4 and 5, and read **Why now** for each: that's your opening line.
4. **Start deals with the good ones.** Select the leads worth a conversation and press **Start a deal**. Say what you're selling. Each one now has a company, a contact and a deal in your [CRM](ttcrm.md).
5. **Get their emails.** Press **Find emails**. This spends credits in Apollo or Clay, so Vendi asks first.
6. **Reach out.** Ask Vendi: "Write a first email to these three, using their Why now." Check each one and send it from your own email.

## Every week after

New leads show on [Today](today.md) under **Needs you**. Filter **All leads** by **No deal yet** to see only the new ones. To change who you're looking for, open the list on the Leads page and press **Change it**.

Tip: A list too broad? Add a reason to buy now, like a new role, a funding round or a hiring push. It narrows the list more than any filter.
