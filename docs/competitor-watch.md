# Competitor watch

News about your competitors, only what's new.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/competitor-watch

Launches, pricing changes, funding, partnerships and changes in how they sell. (Pro)

## Start

Type `/competitors add` and the companies, in the message box or a [chat app](chat-apps.md):

- `/competitors add Gong, Clari, Avoma`
- `/competitors add Acme acme.com` adds the website, so other companies called Acme stay out
- `/competitors AI automations` watches a topic instead of a company

Vendi checks once a day. The first check only learns what's already out there, so your first report comes when something new appears.

## Reports

Reports show in your conversation with Vendi, under **Ran automation › Competitor watch**, and in your chat app if you started there. Each entry has the competitor, what changed, the date, the link and whether it matters for you. Tell Vendi what you sell in your [Playbook](playbook.md) for better takes.

The same news never comes twice, even from another site.

## Commands

| Command | Does |
| --- | --- |
| `/competitors` | Lists what you watch |
| `/competitors add <names>` | Watches more companies |
| `/competitors watch <search>` | Watches your own search |
| `/competitors remove <number>` | Stops one |
| `/competitors daily` | Changes how often: `hourly`, `every 6 hours`, `daily` or `weekly` |
| `/competitors run` | Checks now |
| `/competitors pause`, `resume` or `stop` | Pauses, restarts or stops everything |

Note: Up to 10 companies and searches. Each report counts as one question.
