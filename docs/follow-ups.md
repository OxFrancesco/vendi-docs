# Calls and coaching

Every sales call scored, with the follow-up email drafted.

On the site: https://ttfunnel.ai/docs/follow-ups

Open **CRM › Calls** to see every call from your notetakers over the last three months. Vendi scores your sales calls and tells you what to do next.

Tip: Step by step: [Never miss a follow-up](call-follow-ups.md).

## The call list

Pick a kind above the list, like demo or negotiation, or **Sales** for every sales call. Open a call and press **Score this call** to review it.

Scored calls are tagged by how they ended: **Next step agreed**, **Vague next step**, **No next step** or **Not a sales call**. Calls without a clear next step also show on [Today](today.md).

## The scorecard

- **Call score**, 0 to 100: how far the call moved the deal toward a yes
- **The next step**, with **Draft follow-up email** and **Make it a task**
- **How the selling went**: discovery questions, rapport, tone and how much you talked
- **Where the deal stands**: the problem, the decision maker and the buyer's outlook
- Quotes from the call that back it up

Weak spots link to the matching play in the [Sales Method](playbook.md#sales-method). Calls find their company in the CRM on their own; if one doesn't, press **Link a company**.

## The whole team

**Whole team** shows every seller side by side. Pick a seller to see only their calls.
